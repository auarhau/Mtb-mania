MTB MANIA

ELASTO-STYLE MOUNTAIN BIKE TRIALS
Real bike physics, 13 hand-built levels. Throttle, brake and move your weight — that is the whole game.

Don't smash your helmet. Grab stars. Cross the flag.
▲ / W pedal   ▼ / S brake   ◀ / A ▶ / D lean   Space hop   T turn   R restart   M mute
• Gas and brake act through the tires — hit a wheel and you move.
• Lean (◀ ▶) moves your weight: forward keeps the front down on climbs, back unloads it for manuals and descents. The same world direction no matter which way you face.
• Hop (Space / Shift / J) off lips, over logs, out of trouble.
• Weight and traction: too much lean on a hill and you loop out or spin. Feather it.
• Seesaws: they tip under your weight — keep gas and ride across before the high end drops.
• Air: hold a lean to rotate — full rotation on landing = flip points. Land roughly along the slope or you slam.
• Style pays: flips +500 each (less for repeats); the nearest whole rotation counts, so 2.8 spins = TRIPLE (2000/4500/8000 for doubles/triples/quads), wheelies/stoppies bank points, big air +150.
• Time matters: your final score is the run score × par/time — fast runs with tricks beat long farming runs.
• T turns you around. M mutes. Best time and score are saved per level, and your name goes on the local highscore board.
